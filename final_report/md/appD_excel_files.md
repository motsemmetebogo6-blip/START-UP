D.1 Excel engineering model
:::

The workbook `SAF_Engineering_Model.xlsx` carries the calculations of the electrical analysis, the mass balance and the optimisation as live formulas (264 formulas, no errors), so that the team can change an input and see the result. Its sheets are README, Inputs, MassBalance, Electrical, Optimisation and Verification. The values were checked against the Python model.
